    Where your library lives

    PettiBox has no servers. Your library is kept in three places, all yours:

    • On this computer, inside Chrome, so it works offline and opens instantly.
    • On your phones and tablets with PettiBox.
    • In your own Google Drive, in a "PettiBox" folder, which is how your devices stay in step.

    Nothing passes through anyone else's computers, and we can't see your saves. PettiBox only sees files it made in your Drive, never the rest of it. Saves in locked collections never show here: the browser can't unlock them.

    Keep your own copy as a file on this computer. It opens in any browser and imports into Chrome, Firefox or other bookmark apps.
