## Findings

- "All" sits alone on a second row of the PDF limit buttons on this phone width. Cosmetic.
- The 1953 masthead "КОЧЕГАРКА" (large decorative type) still reads as "KOYerAPKA", and a few lines of the 1916 photo stay Latin junk ("BHBIIHOCTEIO"). Worn, pre-1918 print; most of the page is Cyrillic.
- Known since Nov 2, not new: shared files are titled "Saved PDF" / "Saved image" rather than by file name.
- In Files, a file selected earlier stays selected, so a second share sent both files. Files app behaviour, not PettiBox.
